BUHURT: Steel & Tape public model previews

The equipment and anonymous armoured mannequins are serialized from the live game's own geometry and materials. The game combines procedural geometry with CC0 Quaternius sword and heater-shield assets. Quaternius: https://quaternius.com/

The viewer uses the same Three.js r149 renderer as the game. Three.js is MIT licensed; see ../vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt. Three.js: https://threejs.org/

Preview lighting, camera angles, the display pedestal and inspection pose are wiki presentation. Mannequins use an anonymous 1.8 m body and Reavers colours. These are not player account exports or personalized characters. Only standard, reviewed public equipment is included.
