How to play

  • REG 4.1
    Standing is the score. No health bars. A round stops when one side is outnumbered 3 to 1. When the 5-minute clock runs out, the side with more fighters standing wins.
  • BR 1.3.1
    Grounded means out. Torso, hip, arm, hand, knee or shield on the floor and you sit until "All rise". Lose your helmet and you leave the list.
  • BR 3.1, 3.4
    Hits stagger, trips finish. Strikes and shoves knock fighters off balance. Trip someone who is already stumbling and they go down.
  • BR 4.12, 6
    Control is a skill. No thrusts, no hitting grounded fighters, nothing to the base of the skull. A marshal who sees a foul calls it.
  • Duels
    One on one is scored. In 1v1 and the Duel Ladder, clean hits score: head 3, body 2, arms and legs 1. A takedown scores 2 and the fight goes on. 60-second rounds, best of three. You land where you aim: look up at the helmet for 3 points, low for the legs.
  • Blocks
    Time your guard. Raise it just before the blow lands for a perfect block: no breath spent and the attacker staggers. Holding guard all the time costs breath, and an empty tank gets your guard knocked aside.
  • Off target
    Some places are illegal. The back of the knee, the base of the skull and anyone on the ground are off limits (BR 6). The marshals warn, then card.
  • Bench
    5v5 squads carry three fresh fighters. Blown or battered teammates come off between rounds. Red cards are never replaced: that team fights short.
  • Showboat
    Salute the crowd right after you take someone down (G, R3, or the flashing button) for a roar and a hype boost. Pause for an instant replay of the last takedown from any angle.
  • Every day
    Rumble of the Day is the same fight for everyone on a date, with a twist (rain, heat, tape shortage…). Keep a streak and share your result. Gauntlet is five fights in a row with one life, boons between fights and a code your friends can replay.
  • Fatigue
    You tire as the fight goes on. Every round wears you down: less breath to recover, slower feet and swings, shakier balance. Watch the breath bar and pick your moments.
  • BR 2.1.5
    Tape it up. Between rounds, repair straps with cloth hockey tape. Duct tape holds, but it fails the kit check.

Rules follow the Buhurt International documents (BR 26.4.1, REG 26.4, WR 26.4). Balance, wear and timing numbers are game tuning, not rules.
