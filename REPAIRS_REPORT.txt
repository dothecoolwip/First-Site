Implemented
- Help initializes its controls from every entry point and remembers the input tab.
- One searchable Places & services directory supplies map and vendor navigation. Missing descriptions are omitted; hidden entrances use the marker discovery rules.
- The rejected-account dashboard exposes Sign out, reports retry failures, labels derived matches-minus-wins as Non-wins (including CSV), and names rolling activity windows accurately.
- Dashboard platform totals/raw events are secondary; loaded-period unresolved reports have their own summary.
- Profile and Marta use the same appearance controls. Progress/Awards/Record are grouped; obsolete career panels are removed with stale-tab redirects. Audio diagnostic buttons are in Advanced audio.
- NPCs call shared activity-launch functions. Hidden legacy activity buttons were removed; their activities remain available through town and mode screens.
- Barber option changes update controls and mirrors in place. Body-style changes restore scroll and focus around their necessary structural update. Short-window Profile editor sizing was corrected during verification.
- Every human receives a bounded combat profile. Skills, upgrades, effects, equipment variants, armour quality, recovery, padding, rally/slip, and grappling benefits resolve independently of local camera ownership. Couch humans share the host career with their selected equipment.
- AI decisions use copied observable posture/motion, inferred opponent targeting, estimated stamina, crowd visibility, and expiring observations. Reactive counters retain the 110 ms gate; unseen noise supplies a bearing rather than a strike arc.
- Remote controls are normalized and validated; ordered packets expire after 500 ms and cancel held actions. Inputs are associated with their owning peer. Protocol version 2, action identifiers, and squad-order counters are included.
- Online reward processing is idempotent for the match. Competitive menus/photo keep simulation live; same-side co-op pauses require all connected humans. Menus own and neutralize gameplay input.
- Spectator navigation owns keyboard/controller focus. Shared/split couch views show individual stamina, balance, and action state; contact rumble is sent to the relevant controller.
- Challenge level appears in tactics and the lobby. Guard telemetry distinguishes full/partial/perfect/failed attempts and identifies the fighter; active fight duration follows simulated fight steps.
- A reusable seeded equipped-policy outcome runner records the seven milestones across 1v1, 5v5 and 12v12. It declines emergency repairs through the existing timeout outcome. These exploratory results do not establish skilled human performance.
